## Hikaya learns from your corrections

Fix a dictation in **History** with the pencil button. Hikaya notes the words you changed, and when you make the same fix twice it suggests a rule in **Learning**. Accept it, and that word comes out right every time.

You can also add your own replacement rules in **Learning**: "get hub" becomes "GitHub", a colleague's name is always spelled right.

## English and French in the same sentence

Hesitations are now removed in both languages at once ("euh", "um", "hum"). If you use AI cleanup, the new **Hikaya Cleanup (English + French)** prompt keeps each part of the sentence in the language you spoke. If the AI translates or rewrites anyway, Hikaya pastes your words as spoken instead.

## Your own archive

Connect Hikaya to a hub, a small server on a machine you own, in the new **Sync** section. Every dictation is kept there, encrypted, and your rules follow you from one device to another. Without the hub, everything stays on this computer, as before.
