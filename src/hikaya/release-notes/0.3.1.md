## Meeting notes

The new **Meetings** section records a meeting and writes the transcript as it goes, every 30 seconds.

- Your microphone is shown as **You**, and what your computer plays (the other people on a call) as **Others**. If the computer's sound can't be recorded, Hikaya tells you and records your microphone alone.
- After the meeting, **Summarize** writes a TL;DR, the key points, the decisions, the action items and the open questions, in the meeting's language. It uses the AI provider chosen in Post Processing (Ollama by default).
- Copy or save a meeting as Markdown, or as subtitles (SRT).

Recording people without their agreement can be illegal: tell everyone the meeting is recorded.

If Hikaya is connected to a hub (in **Sync**), each finished meeting, with its transcript and summary, is saved there too.

## Your archive on your phone

Open your hub's address in your phone's browser to search your dictations, read your meetings and their summaries, or unlock the hub after it restarts. On iPhone, **Share → Add to Home Screen** makes it open like an app.
