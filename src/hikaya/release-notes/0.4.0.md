## Record on your phone

Open your hub's page on your phone and tap **Record** for an in-person meeting or a voice note. The audio goes to your hub as you speak. The next time Hikaya runs on your computer, usually within a minute, it transcribes the recording and adds it to **Meetings**, with a summary if an AI provider is set up. Everyone in the room is shown as **Room**. The hub deletes the audio afterwards.

Keep the page open and the screen on while recording. For a quick voice note, an iPhone Shortcut can do the same and copy the text to the clipboard.

## Meeting notes

The **Meetings** section records a meeting and writes the transcript as it goes, every 30 seconds.

- Your microphone is shown as **You**, and what your computer plays (the other people on a call) as **Others**. If the computer's sound can't be recorded, Hikaya tells you and records your microphone alone.
- While a meeting records, the tray shows it, with **Stop**.
- After the meeting, **Summarize** writes a TL;DR, the key points, the decisions, the action items and the open questions, in the meeting's language. It uses the AI provider chosen in Post Processing (Ollama by default).
- Copy or save a meeting as Markdown, or as subtitles (SRT). Meeting audio is deleted with your recordings setting; the transcript stays.

Recording people without their agreement can be illegal: tell everyone the meeting is recorded.

## Hikaya knows your words

The AI cleanup now gets your custom words and the replacements you taught Hikaya, so names and jargon stay spelled your way. **Learning** shows how much you dictated today and this week.

## Your archive on your phone

Open your hub's address in your phone's browser to search your dictations, read your meetings and their summaries, or unlock the hub after it restarts. On iPhone, **Share → Add to Home Screen** makes it open like an app.
