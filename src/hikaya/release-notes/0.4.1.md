## Your words on every computer

Custom words now sync through your hub, like replacement rules. Add a name on one computer and it is added on the others; remove it and it is removed everywhere. Your hub needs the matching update. Until then, the words wait on each computer.

## Problems you can see

When something fails in the background, Hikaya now tells you in the footer instead of only writing it to the log. That covers the sync queue, a meeting with segments that could not be transcribed, and a phone recording that could not be processed.

## Sync that keeps going

If the hub refuses a record (too large, or invalid), Hikaya sets it aside and sends everything queued behind it. The **Sync** screen shows how many records were set aside. Connecting to a different hub sends everything again.

## Smaller fixes

- Recording on the phone resumes its uploads after a dropped connection, and pauses them if the hub no longer accepts the phone.
- `--toggle-meeting` now starts a meeting when Hikaya isn't running yet, so a desktop shortcut works on its first press.
- Hikaya's tray icon has its own ID, so your desktop remembers its settings separately from Handy's.
- Includes Handy 0.10.2: a newer speech engine, and fixes for local AI providers and Linux audio.

New in 0.4.0, if you skipped it: record meetings on your phone from your hub's page, with **Record**.
