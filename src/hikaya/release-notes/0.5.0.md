## Edit by voice

Select text anywhere, hold **Win+Alt+Space** and say what to do: “make it shorter”, “turn this into a list”, “traduis en anglais”. The selection is replaced by the result. It uses the AI model chosen in Post Processing, so it works offline with Ollama. If the model answers in another language than your text when you didn’t ask for a translation, Hikaya leaves your text alone. The shortcut can be changed in **Learning → Command mode**.

## Voice commands

Say **“new line”** or **“à la ligne”**, **“new paragraph”** or **“nouveau paragraphe”**, and **“delete that”** or **“supprime ça”** to remove the sentence you just said. Pause briefly before and after, so Hikaya can tell a command from a sentence like “I added a new line of code”.

## Written for the app you’re in

On Windows, Hikaya notes which app you dictate into. In a chat (Slack, Teams, WhatsApp…) the cleanup keeps it casual, without a period after one short sentence. In a terminal, commands stay in lowercase. In a code editor, code words and file names are kept as spoken. Replacement rules can now apply to one app only. Turn it off in **Learning → Adapt to the app**.

## Snippets

Save a text under a short cue in **Learning → Snippets**, such as “my address” or “mon lien calendly”. Say the cue while dictating, and Hikaya types the text, line breaks included. Snippets sync to your other computers through your hub.

Your hub needs the matching update for snippets to sync. Until then they wait on each computer.

New in 0.4, if you skipped it: record meetings on your phone from your hub’s page with **Record**, and custom words that sync between your computers.
